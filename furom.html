Our Community Values

1. Radical Inclusivity

Every voice matters. This platform is built to be fully accessible for users of all abilities, backgrounds, and perspectives.

2. Respectful Discourse

Passionate debate is welcome, but personal attacks, hate speech, and harassment are not. Critique ideas, not people.

3. Authenticity & Integrity

Share genuine, thoughtful contributions. Help us keep this space honest and free from misleading or harmful content.

4. Open Participation

Everyone is invited to share their thoughts, reply to discussions, and start new conversations on topics that matter to you.

5. Collective Responsibility

We all help keep this space safe. Uphold these values, and report content that violates our community guidelines.
